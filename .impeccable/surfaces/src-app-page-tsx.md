Scope: home page (`/`), Persuade mode. Logged-out visitors: mostly creators who want to reuse other creators' clips; creators who want to earn come second.

Job: show that the videos here can be licensed, give two clear actions (browse videos, sell your videos), explain licensing in three steps, invite creators, close on the brand line.

Constraints: white background, spacious, never overwhelming (user brief). Real photos and video only (Unsplash and Pexels placeholders for now). No invented traction; the "Licence valid" card uses seeded demo data.

Approved: variant D ("Blend") at design/variants/d.html, 10 Nov 2026. The earlier "Proof of Payment" receipt direction was rejected as cluttered.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The videos do the talking. A centred, calm page that leads with a playing creator video and refuses the dense marketplace layout (rows of cards, pricing tables, explanation blocks).
OWN-WORLD: White page, Ink type, one blue used only as small marks. Bricolage Grotesque headlines, Figtree text. Large rounded inset media frames, clip tiles that play muted previews on hover, white pill buttons on media and ink pills on white.
STORY: The visitor sees a creator video and the promise "License videos from creators you love", browses by category, learns the three steps, sees that creators keep 85%, and leaves on "Your work. Your mark. Your money."
FIRST VIEWPORT: A rounded inset frame filling the first screen with a muted looping creator video under a 50% scrim; centred display headline, one lede line, white "Browse videos" and outlined "Sell your videos" buttons; small pause control in the corner.
FORM: Blend of the Centered (Selar-like) structure with the Big picture (Patreon-like) video hero and closing band, chosen by the user over three variants. Original seed key 97128a4a (that roll's direction was rejected).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
